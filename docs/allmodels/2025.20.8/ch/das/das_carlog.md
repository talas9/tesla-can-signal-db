`DAS_carLogMultiplexer` is the multiplexer selector of this message. Its value picks which group of signals is valid in a frame: page 1 (3 signals), page 2 (16 signals), page 3 (4 signals), page 4 (5 signals). Signals without a page are present in every frame.
