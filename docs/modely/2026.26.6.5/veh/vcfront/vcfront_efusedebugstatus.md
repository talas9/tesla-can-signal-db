## Multiplexing

`VCFRONT_eFuseDebugStatusIndex` is the multiplexer selector of this message. Its value picks which group of signals is valid in a frame: page 0 (5 signals), page 1 (12 signals), page 2 (11 signals), page 3 (13 signals), page 4 (13 signals), page 5 (12 signals), page 6 (12 signals), page 7 (5 signals), page 8 (3 signals), page 9 (5 signals), page 10 (4 signals), page 12 (6 signals), page 13 (6 signals), page 14 (4 signals), page 15 (4 signals), page 16 (2 signals), page 17 (3 signals). Signals without a page are present in every frame.
