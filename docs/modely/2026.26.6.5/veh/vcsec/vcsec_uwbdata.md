## Multiplexing

`VCSEC_UWBEndpointIndex` is the multiplexer selector of this message. Its value picks which group of signals is valid in a frame: page 0 (7 signals), page 1 (5 signals), page 2 (5 signals), page 3 (5 signals), page 4 (5 signals). Signals without a page are present in every frame.
