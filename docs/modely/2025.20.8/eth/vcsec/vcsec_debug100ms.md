## Multiplexing

`VCSEC_debug100msIndex` is the multiplexer selector of this message. Its value picks which group of signals is valid in a frame: page 0 (15 signals), page 7 (6 signals), page 8 (10 signals), page 9 (8 signals). Signals without a page are present in every frame.
